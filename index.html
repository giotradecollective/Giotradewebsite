Protection of Personal Information Policy

Issued in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, No. 4 of 2013 (POPIA)

1. Regulatory Commitment

GIOTRADE Investment Solutions LLC (“GIOTRADE” or “the Firm”) is committed to the lawful, transparent, and responsible processing of personal information in full compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, No. 4 of 2013 (“POPIA” or “the Act”), as administered by the Information Regulator of South Africa.

As an investment management firm, GIOTRADE recognizes that the responsible handling of client and stakeholder information is fundamental to maintaining trust, ensuring regulatory compliance, and safeguarding client interests.

2. Scope of Application

This Policy applies to all personal information processed by GIOTRADE in the course of:

  • Investment management mandates and portfolio administration
  • Client onboarding, identity verification, and due diligence
  • Portfolio monitoring, performance reporting, and client communications
  • Internal risk management, compliance, and operational processes
  • Interactions with third-party service providers, regulators, and counterparties
3. Definitions

“Personal Information” means information relating to an identifiable, living, natural person or an identifiable, existing juristic person, including but not limited to:

  • Identity and biometric verification information
  • Contact details and correspondence records
  • Financial data, banking details, and source of funds declarations
  • Investment objectives, risk tolerance, and portfolio holdings
  • Transactional and account-related records
  • Employment, income, and economic profile information

“Processing” means any operation or activity, automated or not, concerning personal information, including collection, receipt, recording, organization, storage, updating, distribution, or destruction.

“Responsible Party” means GIOTRADE Investment Solutions LLC, which determines the purpose and means of processing personal information.

4. Lawful Basis for Processing

GIOTRADE processes personal information strictly within the lawful bases prescribed under POPIA, including:

  • Contractual necessity: for the execution and administration of investment mandates
  • Legal and regulatory: obligations compliance, reporting, and record-keeping requirements
  • Legitimate interests: risk management, fraud prevention, and operational efficiency
  • Consent of the data subject: where required and where no other lawful basis applies
5. FICA and Know Your Client (KYC) Compliance

In accordance with the Financial Intelligence Centre Act, No. 38 of 2001 (“FICA”), GIOTRADE implements rigorous Know Your Client (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) procedures as part of its client onboarding and ongoing due diligence obligations.

The Firm is legally obligated to report certain transactions or activities to the Financial Intelligence Centre (“FIC”) where required under FICA, including suspicious or unusual transactions. Clients are advised that such reporting may occur without prior notification where legally mandated.
6. Rights of Data Subjects

In accordance with POPIA, data subjects have the following rights:

  • The right to access personal information held by GIOTRADE
  • The right to request correction or deletion of inaccurate, irrelevant, or outdated information
  • The right to object to the processing of personal information
  • The right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • The right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa
