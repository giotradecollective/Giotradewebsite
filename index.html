Legal & Compliance

Protection of Personal Information Policy

Issued in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, No. 4 of 2013 (POPIA)

1. Regulatory Commitment

GIOTRADE Investment Solutions LLC ("GIOTRADE" or "the Firm") is committed to the lawful, transparent, and responsible processing of personal information in full compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, No. 4 of 2013 ("POPIA" or "the Act"), as administered by the Information Regulator of South Africa.

As an investment management firm, GIOTRADE recognizes that the responsible handling of client and stakeholder information is fundamental to maintaining trust, ensuring regulatory compliance, and safeguarding client interests.

2. Scope of Application

This Policy applies to all personal information processed by GIOTRADE in the course of:

  • Investment management mandates and portfolio administration
  • Client onboarding, identity verification, and due diligence
  • Portfolio monitoring, performance reporting, and client communications
  • Internal risk management, compliance, and operational processes
  • Interactions with third-party service providers, regulators, and counterparties
3. Definitions

"Personal Information" means information relating to an identifiable, living, natural person or an identifiable, existing juristic person, including but not limited to:

  • Identity and biometric verification information
  • Contact details and correspondence records
  • Financial data, banking details, and source of funds declarations
  • Investment objectives, risk tolerance, and portfolio holdings
  • Transactional and account-related records
  • Employment, income, and economic profile information

"Processing" means any operation or activity, automated or not, concerning personal information, including collection, receipt, recording, organization, storage, updating, distribution, or destruction.

"Responsible Party" means GIOTRADE Investment Solutions LLC, which determines the purpose and means of processing personal information.

"Data Subject" means the natural or juristic person to whom personal information relates.

4. Lawful Basis for Processing

GIOTRADE processes personal information strictly within the lawful bases prescribed under POPIA, including:

  • Contractual necessity: for the execution and administration of investment mandates
  • Legal and regulatory: obligations compliance, reporting, and record-keeping requirements
  • Legitimate interests: risk management, fraud prevention, and operational efficiency, subject to balancing against data subject rights
  • Consent of the data subject: where required and where no other lawful basis applies
5. Purpose Specification and Limitation

Personal information is collected and processed for clearly defined, specific, and legitimate purposes, including:

  • Structuring and managing client investment positions and portfolios
  • Performing portfolio analysis, performance measurement, and client reporting
  • Conducting risk profiling and investment suitability assessments
  • Executing investment strategies and capital allocation decisions
  • Maintaining internal audit trails and compliance records
  • Enhancing proprietary analytics, reporting systems, and operational infrastructure

Personal information will not be processed for purposes incompatible with those for which it was originally collected, unless the data subject consents or processing is required by law.

6. FICA and Know Your Client (KYC) Compliance

In accordance with the Financial Intelligence Centre Act, No. 38 of 2001 ("FICA"), GIOTRADE implements rigorous Know Your Client (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) procedures as part of its client onboarding and ongoing due diligence obligations.

6.1 Client Identification and Verification

The Firm is required to verify the identity of all clients prior to establishing a business relationship. Required documentation includes:

  • Valid government-issued identification document (identity document or passport)
  • Proof of residential or registered address
  • Source of funds and source of wealth declaration
  • Banking details verification

6.2 Risk-Based Due Diligence

  • Enhanced due diligence for clients identified as higher risk
  • Ongoing monitoring of client activity, transactional patterns, and investment behavior
  • Periodic review and update of client information to ensure accuracy and currency

6.3 Record-Keeping Obligations

GIOTRADE retains records of client identification and verification documents, transactional and portfolio-related information, and communication records for the minimum period prescribed by applicable law.

6.4 Reporting Obligations

The Firm is legally obligated to report certain transactions or activities to the Financial Intelligence Centre ("FIC") where required under FICA, including suspicious or unusual transactions. Clients are advised that such reporting may occur without prior notification where legally mandated, and that the Firm is prohibited by law from disclosing that a report has been made ("tipping off").
7. Information Quality and Minimization

GIOTRADE ensures that all personal information processed is adequate, relevant, and not excessive; accurate and kept up to date; limited to what is strictly necessary for the specified purpose; and corrected or deleted where found to be inaccurate or no longer necessary.

8. Information Security Safeguards

The Firm implements appropriate technical and organizational safeguards, including:

  • Encrypted data storage and secure information infrastructure
  • Role-based access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel only
  • Continuous system monitoring, vulnerability assessments, and security audits
  • Confidential handling of all financial, portfolio, and client-sensitive data
  • Staff training and awareness on data protection obligations
9. Data Retention and Record-Keeping

Personal information is retained only for as long as is necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to the duration of the client relationship, applicable legal and regulatory retention requirements (including FICA and POPIA), and GIOTRADE's internal records management policy. Upon expiry of the applicable retention period, personal information is securely destroyed, deleted, or de-identified.

10. Disclosure and Third-Party Processing

GIOTRADE may disclose personal information to third parties only where necessary and lawful, including financial institutions, prime brokers, regulatory authorities, and approved third-party service providers operating under binding confidentiality and data processing agreements. All third-party disclosures are subject to strict confidentiality obligations.

11. Cross-Border Transfers of Personal Information

Where personal information is transferred to a recipient located in a foreign country, GIOTRADE ensures that the recipient country provides an adequate level of protection equivalent to POPIA, or that adequate contractual, technical, and organizational safeguards are in place, or that the data subject has consented to the transfer.

12. Rights of Data Subjects

In accordance with POPIA, data subjects have the following rights:

  • The right to access personal information held by GIOTRADE
  • The right to request correction or deletion of inaccurate, irrelevant, or outdated information
  • The right to object to the processing of personal information
  • The right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • The right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

14. Incident Response and Breach Notification

In the event of a security compromise involving personal information, GIOTRADE will immediately investigate and contain the incident, assess the nature and scope, notify the Information Regulator as soon as reasonably practicable, and notify affected data subjects where the compromise is likely to result in serious harm.

15. Governance, Review, and Policy Updates

This Policy forms part of GIOTRADE's broader compliance and governance framework and will be reviewed at least annually, or upon any material change in law, regulation, or the Firm's processing activities. Material updates will be communicated to affected data subjects and stakeholders as appropriate.

16. Acceptance and Acknowledgment

If you do not agree, please contact the Information Officer prior to engagement.
