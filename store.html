Frequently Asked Questions
What platforms are the tools compatible with? +
Our indicators are built for TradingView and MetaTrader 5 (MT5). The Pro Dashboard and Equity Scanner are web-based and accessible from any device. The Risk & Position Tracker is available on both web and mobile.
Can I try a product before purchasing? +
We offer a 7-day demo access on all subscription-based products. One-time purchase indicators can be trialled in a paper trading environment before live deployment. Contact our team to arrange a demo session.
Do the tools work for beginners or only experienced traders? +
Our tools are designed to be genuinely useful at every level. Each product includes setup guides, usage videos, and access to our support team. That said, we recommend a working understanding of the relevant market before using any trading tool.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept all major debit and credit cards, EFT, and select cryptocurrency payments. All transactions are secured via our payment gateway. South African clients can also pay via Ozow and Peach Payments.
What is your refund policy? +
Subscription products can be cancelled at any time with no penalty. For one-time purchases, we offer a 14-day satisfaction guarantee — if the tool doesn’t perform as described, we will issue a full refund or provide an equivalent credit toward another product.
Will I receive updates after purchasing? +
Subscription-based products receive all updates automatically. One-time purchase products receive free minor updates for 12 months from purchase date. Major version upgrades are made available to existing users at a significantly discounted rate.
